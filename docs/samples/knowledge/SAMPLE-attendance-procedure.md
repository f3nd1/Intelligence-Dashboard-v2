# Recording Attendance

Lecturers record attendance at the start of each session. A student arriving
more than fifteen (15) minutes after the start is recorded as late; three
late marks count as one absence.

# Intervention

Where attendance falls below the threshold, Student Services contacts the
student within five (5) working days and records the contact in the Student
Log. Persistent non-attendance is escalated to the Academic Board.

# Approved Leave

Leave approved in advance does not count towards the absence total. The
approval must be recorded before the affected session.
