# Refund Eligibility

A student may request a refund within FOURTEEN (14) days of course
commencement. Requests after this window are considered only where
exceptional circumstances apply and are approved by the Academic Board.

# Refund Calculation

Where a refund is approved, the amount returned is the fees paid less an
administrative charge. The administrative charge in this SAMPLE document is
ten percent (10%) of the total course fee.

# Withdrawal Procedure

A withdrawal must be submitted in writing to Student Services. The Registrar
confirms the withdrawal within five (5) working days and notifies the
Finance team so any refund can be processed.

# Appeals

A student who disagrees with a refund decision may appeal in writing within
thirty (30) days. Appeals are heard by a panel that did not make the
original decision.
